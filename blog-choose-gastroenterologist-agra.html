How to Choose a Gastroenterologist in Agra

“Best” is not a medical qualification. Objective criteria are more useful.

Searches for the “best gastroenterologist in Agra” are common, but the word “best” is subjective. A better approach is to look at qualifications, registration, relevance to your condition and whether the clinic provides the investigations or procedures you may actually need.

Look for recognised specialist training

For complex digestive and liver disease, super-speciality gastroenterology training is relevant. Dr. Krishna Kohli holds DM Medical Gastroenterology (Gold Medalist), KGMU, after MD Internal Medicine.

Match the specialist to the problem

A patient with fatty liver or hepatitis may value hepatology-focused care, while another patient may need endoscopy, inflammatory bowel disease management or pancreatic disease assessment.

Communication and follow-up matter

A useful consultation should explain the likely diagnosis, what tests are necessary, what can safely wait and how follow-up will be organised.
