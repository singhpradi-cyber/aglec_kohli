SYMPTOMS & FOLLOW-UP

Consultation for digestive concerns

Similar symptoms can have different causes. A clinical assessment helps decide what to investigate.

Acidity & reflux

Discuss recurrent heartburn, reflux or upper-abdominal discomfort and how symptoms relate to meals and medicines.

IBS, bloating & constipation

Review recurrent bloating, abdominal discomfort and changing bowel habits. IBS is assessed after considering other possible causes.

IBD & ongoing bowel care

Review ulcerative colitis or Crohn’s disease concerns, previous investigations and ongoing treatment. IBD and IBS are different conditions.

Pancreas care

Assessment and treatment planning for pancreatitis and other pancreatic concerns. Bring any previous scans, blood tests and treatment records.

Gallbladder & bile-duct care

Review gallbladder symptoms, scans and bile-duct concerns. Discuss treatment options and whether a procedure or surgical referral is needed.

Appendix assessment

Evaluation of appendix-related concerns and guidance on appropriate treatment or surgical referral. Severe or worsening abdominal pain needs urgent assessment.

Symptoms that need prompt care

Rectal bleeding or unexplained weight loss needs medical assessment. For severe abdominal pain, heavy or continuous bleeding, or vomiting blood, seek emergency care rather than waiting for a clinic appointment.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before you book

Do I need an endoscopy before booking?

No. Bring any reports you already have. The doctor will decide whether an endoscopy or other investigation is appropriate after assessing your symptoms.

Are IBS and IBD the same?

No. They are different conditions, although some symptoms overlap. A consultation can help decide which investigations and follow-up are appropriate.
