LIVER ASSESSMENT

What does a FibroScan measure?

A non-invasive scan provides information that your doctor interprets alongside your history, blood tests and other reports.

Liver stiffness

The stiffness reading, reported in kPa, helps assess the likelihood of liver scarring. Inflammation and other factors can affect the result, so a single number is not a complete diagnosis.

Liver fat

Where CAP measurement is included, it estimates fatty change in the liver. Ask reception what measurements are included in your scan.

Report review

Dr. Kohli can discuss the result in the context of fatty liver, hepatitis or another liver condition and advise on further assessment or follow-up.

IS IT APPROPRIATE FOR YOU?

Discuss your liver findings

A doctor may recommend liver stiffness assessment after fatty liver is found on a scan, for persistently abnormal liver blood tests, or during follow-up of chronic liver disease.

The right investigation depends on your history and examination. Bring previous ultrasound reports, blood tests, FibroScan results and your medicine list.

YOUR QUESTIONS

FibroScan appointment questions

What happens during the scan?

You lie on an examination couch while an operator places a probe on the skin over the liver. You may feel brief vibrations. The test does not involve a needle or sedation.

Does FibroScan replace every other liver test?

No. It adds information about liver stiffness and, where measured, fat. Your doctor interprets it with your medical history and other tests and may recommend further investigations.

How much does a FibroScan cost?

Reception will confirm the current fee and what it includes. Call before travelling; this website does not take payment or reserve a scan automatically.
