LIVER HEALTH

Understand your liver reports

Bring ultrasound reports, previous liver blood tests, prescriptions and any earlier specialist advice.

Fatty liver assessment

Review scan findings alongside metabolic risk factors and medical history. Further assessment of liver scarring may be advised when appropriate.

Abnormal LFTs & hepatitis

Discuss raised liver enzymes, jaundice or hepatitis reports in the context of your symptoms, medicines and earlier results.

Cirrhosis & chronic liver disease

Consult for review of known liver disease, monitoring and follow-up planning based on your existing records.

FIBROSCAN ENQUIRIES

Discuss liver stiffness assessment

A liver consultation can help determine whether liver elastography or another investigation is useful. An ultrasound report alone does not answer every question about liver health.

Ask reception to confirm FibroScan availability, location, cost and preparation before travelling. Booking a consultation does not automatically book a test.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before you book

What should I bring to a liver consultation?

Bring ultrasound or other scan reports, liver blood tests, current medicines and records of any earlier liver treatment.

Does fatty liver always cause symptoms?

It may be found on tests done for another reason. Dr. Kohli can review the finding with your medical history and discuss whether further assessment is appropriate.

Can I book a FibroScan through this page?

Use the contact buttons to enquire. Reception must confirm availability, location, preparation and the fee; this page does not reserve a procedure slot.
