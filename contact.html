YOUR QUESTIONS

Before you book

What are Dr. Kohli’s OPD hours?

Dr. Krishna Kohli is available in OPD from 10 AM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday (India Standard Time). OPD is closed on Sunday. Call reception to confirm your appointment.

Is IPD available on Sundays?

Yes. IPD is available 24 hours a day, every day, including Sundays. These inpatient hours are separate from Dr. Kohli’s OPD consultation hours.

Where is the clinic?

AGLEC is at Radha Rani Complex, near Bhagat Halwai, opposite Axis Bank, Kargil Petrol Pump, Agra. Use the directions button to open Dr. Kohli’s Maps listing and confirm the entrance with reception.

Can I confirm the consultation fee online?

The fee is not published here. Reception can confirm the current consultation fee and any separate test or procedure charges.

Does a WhatsApp message confirm my appointment?

No. Wait for reception to confirm a date and time. You do not need to send a detailed medical history to request a slot.
